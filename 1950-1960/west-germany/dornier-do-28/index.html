Dornier Do 28

Manufacturer: Dornier GmbH
Country / decade: West Germany, 1950–1960
Category: Civil & military utility
Role: Twin-engine STOL utility aircraft
First flight: 29 April 1959
Entered service: early 1960s

Where it was used, and by whom

Developed from the single-engine Do 27, the twin-engine Do 28 served primarily with the West German Luftwaffe and Marineflieger for liaison and light transport, alongside civil operators.

Notes

The Do 28 extended Dornier's STOL utility-aircraft line into a twin-engine design, continuing the company's specialism established with the Do 27 earlier in the decade.
