Dornier Do 27

Manufacturer: Dornier GmbH (Germany); early production also by CASA (Spain)
Country / decade: West Germany, 1950–1960
Category: Military & civil utility
Role: Short take-off and landing (STOL) light utility aircraft
First flight: 27 June 1955 (German-built prototype)
Entered service: 1956, with the West German Luftwaffe

Where it was used, and by whom

The West German Luftwaffe was the primary operator, with roughly 570 aircraft built in Germany. It also served with the Belgian and Swiss air forces, the Israeli Air Force (locally nicknamed “Dror”), and the Spanish and Portuguese air forces.

Notes

The Do 27 was the first aircraft built in quantity in West Germany after the Second World War, at a time when German aircraft manufacturing was still legally restricted. Its design traces back to the Do 25, developed by Claude Dornier's design office in Spain to meet a Spanish military requirement — an early example of cross-border cooperation even before West Germany's industry had formally restarted.
