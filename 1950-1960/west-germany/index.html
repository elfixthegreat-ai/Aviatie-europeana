West Germany · 1950–1960

Barred from building military aircraft until 1955, West Germany's industry restarted almost from zero, producing its first postwar aircraft — a small utility type — only in the middle of the decade.

Aircraft types covered

  • Dornier Do 27 Military & civil utility — Short take-off and landing (STOL) light utility aircraft
  • Dornier Do 28 Civil & military utility — Twin-engine STOL utility aircraft
