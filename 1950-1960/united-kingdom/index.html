United Kingdom · 1950–1960

Britain entered the 1950s as the world's most advanced civil jet manufacturer, and its factories also supplied a stream of first-generation jet fighters and bombers to the RAF and NATO allies.

Aircraft types covered

  • de Havilland Comet Civil — Jet airliner — the world's first to enter commercial service
  • Hawker Hunter Military — Day fighter, later fighter-bomber
