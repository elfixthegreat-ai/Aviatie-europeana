Fokker F27 Friendship

Manufacturer: Fokker
Country / decade: Netherlands, 1950–1960
Category: Civil
Role: Twin-engine turboprop regional airliner
First flight: 24 November 1955
Entered service: 19 November 1958

Where it was used, and by whom

The F27 was sold to airlines worldwide, including numerous European carriers and, through a Fairchild-built American version, many United States local-service airlines. It went on to become the best-selling European turboprop airliner of its era.

Notes

With 586 aircraft built in the Netherlands (plus further Fairchild-built examples in the United States), the F27 was the most numerous postwar aircraft manufactured in the Netherlands, launching Fokker's reputation as a specialist in rugged, regional airliners.
