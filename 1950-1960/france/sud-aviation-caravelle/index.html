Sud Aviation Caravelle

Manufacturer: Sud Aviation (developed by SNCASE)
Country / decade: France, 1950–1960
Category: Civil
Role: Short/medium-haul jet airliner — the first of its kind
First flight: 27 May 1955
Entered service: 26 April 1959, with Scandinavian Airlines System (SAS)

Where it was used, and by whom

Air France was the launch customer and a long-term operator; SAS, Swissair, and Finnair were also early adopters. Over its production life the Caravelle flew with more than a hundred airlines across Europe (Sabena, Iberia, TAP Air Portugal, Alitalia, Austrian Airlines), Africa (Air Algérie, Air Afrique, Royal Air Maroc), Asia (Indian Airlines, Thai Airways), and South America (Aerolíneas Argentinas, VARIG).

Notes

The Caravelle was the first airliner designed specifically for short and medium-haul routes, and its rear-mounted engine layout was widely copied by later designs. Some 282 were built between 1958 and 1972.
