Saab 35 Draken

Manufacturer: Saab AB
Country / decade: Sweden, 1960–1970
Category: Military
Role: Supersonic interceptor and fighter
First flight: 25 October 1955
Entered service: 6 March 1960, with the Swedish Air Force

Where it was used, and by whom

The Swedish Air Force operated 651 aircraft. The type was exported to Denmark, Finland, and Austria, and remained in Austrian service until 2005.

Notes

The Draken's distinctive “double delta” wing shape was developed to combine high supersonic speed with manageable low-speed handling for operations from Sweden's dispersed road-based airfield system.
