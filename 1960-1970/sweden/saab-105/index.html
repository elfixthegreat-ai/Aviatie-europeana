Saab 105

Manufacturer: Saab AB
Country / decade: Sweden, 1960–1970
Category: Military
Role: Twin-jet trainer and light attack aircraft
First flight: 29 June 1963
Entered service: 1967, with the Swedish Air Force (as Sk 60)

Where it was used, and by whom

The Swedish Air Force bought 150 as the Sk 60, replacing the de Havilland Vampire; the only export customer was Austria, which bought 40 as the Saab 105Ö and used it both for training and, briefly, air defence.

Notes

Developed as a private venture by Saab rather than to a government specification, the 105 remained in Swedish service for 57 years, finally retiring in 2024.
