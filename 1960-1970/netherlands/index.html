Netherlands · 1960–1970

The F27 reached its widest international sales during this decade, sold to airlines across Europe and, through a Fairchild-built American version, to local-service carriers throughout the United States — an unusually broad reach for a Dutch-designed civil aircraft.

Dutch industry also joined the multinational effort to build the American-designed F-104G Starfighter for NATO air forces, with Fokker taking a share of production alongside Messerschmitt and Dornier in West Germany, Fiat in Italy, and SABCA in Belgium — placing the Netherlands inside the largest multinational fighter-production programme of the decade, even though the underlying design was not its own. Toward the end of the decade, Fokker also began work on a jet-powered successor to the F27, the F28, which first flew in 1967.
