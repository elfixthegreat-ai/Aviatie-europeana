PZL TS-11 Iskra

Manufacturer: PZL-Mielec
Country / decade: Poland, 1960–1970
Category: Military
Role: Jet trainer
First flight: 5 February 1960
Entered service: 1964, with the Polish Air Force

Where it was used, and by whom

The Polish Air Force and Polish Navy were the sole military operators within the Warsaw Pact — Poland chose to keep its own design after the L-29 was selected as the bloc-wide standard. India later bought 76 aircraft in the 1970s and 1990s.

Notes

The Iskra was Poland's first domestically designed jet aircraft, powered by an equally home-grown engine, the PZL SO-1/SO-3. It remained in Polish military service for more than 50 years, into the 2020s.
