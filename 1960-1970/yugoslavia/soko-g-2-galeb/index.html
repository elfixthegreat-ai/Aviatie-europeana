SOKO G-2 Galeb

Manufacturer: SOKO, Mostar
Country / decade: Yugoslavia, 1960–1970
Category: Military
Role: Jet trainer and light ground-attack aircraft
First flight: 3 July 1961
Entered service: 1965, with the Yugoslav Air Force

Where it was used, and by whom

The Yugoslav Air Force used the Galeb as its standard jet trainer, replacing American T-33s; Libya became the largest export customer with more than 100 aircraft, and smaller numbers went to Zambia, Zaire, and Indonesia.

Notes

The Galeb was the first jet aircraft series-manufactured in Yugoslavia, reflecting the country's non-aligned Cold War posture and its policy of domestic defence self-sufficiency rather than depending on either NATO or Warsaw Pact suppliers. A total of 248 were built.
