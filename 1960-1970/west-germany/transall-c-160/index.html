Transall C-160

Manufacturer: Transall consortium (Nord Aviation of France; Weser Flugzeugbau / Hamburger Flugzeugbau of West Germany)
Country / decade: West Germany, 1960–1970
Category: Military
Role: Twin-turboprop tactical transport
First flight: 25 February 1963
Entered service: 1967, with the French and West German air forces

Where it was used, and by whom

Developed jointly to replace the piston-engined Nord Noratlas in both the French Armée de l'Air and the West German Luftwaffe, the C-160 later found export customers in South Africa and Turkey, the latter still operating the type into the 2020s.

Notes

“Transall” abbreviates Transporter Allianz, the Franco-German consortium formed in 1959. Italy was briefly involved in the early programme before withdrawing to build its own smaller Fiat G.222. A total of 214 C-160s were built.
