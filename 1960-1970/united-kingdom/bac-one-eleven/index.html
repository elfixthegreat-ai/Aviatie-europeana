BAC One-Eleven

Manufacturer: British Aircraft Corporation
Country / decade: United Kingdom, 1960–1970
Category: Civil
Role: Short-range jet airliner
First flight: 20 August 1963
Entered service: 1965, with British United Airways

Where it was used, and by whom

British United Airways was the launch customer; American Airlines and Braniff International brought the type to the United States, and it went on to serve with airlines including Aer Lingus, Dan-Air, and British Caledonian. Production later included nine aircraft license-built in Romania as the Rombac One-Eleven.

Notes

The One-Eleven was the second short-haul jet airliner to enter service, after the French Caravelle. Total production reached 244 aircraft (plus the Romanian-built examples); the last active example, used as an airborne testbed, was retired only in 2019.
