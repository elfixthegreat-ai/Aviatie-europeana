English Electric Lightning

Manufacturer: English Electric / British Aircraft Corporation
Country / decade: United Kingdom, 1960–1970
Category: Military
Role: Supersonic interceptor
First flight: 4 August 1954 (P.1A prototype)
Entered service: 1960, with the Royal Air Force

Where it was used, and by whom

The Royal Air Force was the sole major operator, using the Lightning as its principal home-defence interceptor from 1960 until retirement in 1988. It was also exported to the Royal Saudi Air Force and the Kuwait Air Force. RAF Lightnings were, on at least one occasion, used to intercept high-flying American U-2 reconnaissance aircraft, and later served as practice targets against the supersonic Concorde during its flight-test programme.

Notes

The Lightning was the only all-British fighter ever to reach Mach 2 in service, distinguished by its two engines mounted one above the other rather than side-by-side. It was renowned for a near-vertical take-off climb. A total of 337 were built.
