United Kingdom · 1960–1970

British industry moved to supersonic combat aircraft in this decade, while continuing to develop the airliner and V/STOL programmes that would define the 1970s.

Aircraft types covered

  • English Electric Lightning Military — Supersonic interceptor
  • BAC One-Eleven Civil — Short-range jet airliner
