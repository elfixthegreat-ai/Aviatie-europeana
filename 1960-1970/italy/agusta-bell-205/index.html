Agusta-Bell AB 204 / AB 205

Manufacturer: Agusta (under license from Bell Helicopter, USA)
Country / decade: Italy, 1960–1970
Category: Civil & military
Role: Utility transport helicopter
First flight: Bell design first flew 1956; Agusta license production from 1964–65
Entered service: 1965

Design and development

Agusta's license agreement with Bell gave Italian industry access to a proven American helicopter design and its manufacturing know-how at a point when Italy had little independent rotorcraft expertise of its own — a faster route to a domestic helicopter industry than attempting an indigenous design from scratch, mirroring the approach several smaller European air forces took toward fixed-wing fighters in the same period. The AB 205's Vietnam-proven Bell UH-1 lineage made it an immediately credible utility helicopter for European operators from its first deliveries.

Where it was used, and by whom

The Italian armed forces were the principal customer, but Agusta-built examples were also delivered to Austria, Sweden, the Netherlands, and Turkey, and Agusta exported worldwide from its Italian production line.

Notes

Agusta's license-built version of Bell's “Huey” family became one of the foundations of Italy's postwar helicopter industry; the AB 205 (546 built) was more successful commercially than the earlier AB 204.
