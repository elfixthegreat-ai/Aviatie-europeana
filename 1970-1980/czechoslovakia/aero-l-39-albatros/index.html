Aero L-39 Albatros

Manufacturer: Aero Vodochody
Country / decade: Czechoslovakia, 1970–1980
Category: Military
Role: Jet trainer and light attack aircraft
First flight: 4 November 1968
Entered service: 28 March 1972, with the Czechoslovak Air Force

Where it was used, and by whom

Adopted from 1972 as the preferred primary jet trainer across most of the Warsaw Pact, replacing the earlier L-29; the Soviet Air Force alone eventually operated thousands. Post-Cold-War, Ukrainian and other air forces continued flying the type for decades.

Notes

The L-39 was the first trainer aircraft in the world fitted with a turbofan rather than a turbojet engine. Around 2,900 were built in total, making it, with the L-29, one of the two most numerous Cold War jet trainers.
