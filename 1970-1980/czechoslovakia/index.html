Czechoslovakia · 1970–1980

Aero Vodochody's L-39 Albatros, first flown in 1968 and in Czechoslovak Air Force service from 1972, was designed from the outset to succeed the L-29 as the bloc-wide standard trainer, rather than growing into that role afterward as the L-29 effectively had. It became the preferred primary jet trainer across most of the Warsaw Pact.

By this point Aero Vodochody was, by some assessments, the largest single aircraft manufacturing programme anywhere in COMECON outside the Soviet Union itself — a remarkable position for a company based in a country of Czechoslovakia's modest size, achieved specifically because its trainers were designed for bloc-wide adoption rather than purely domestic use.
