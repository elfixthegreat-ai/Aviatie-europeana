Italy · 1970–1980

Italian manufacturers, now consolidated as Aeritalia, moved into tactical military transport aircraft built to NATO requirements.

Aircraft types covered

  • Aeritalia G.222 Military — Medium short take-off and landing (STOL) tactical transport
  • Panavia Tornado Military — Variable-sweep-wing strike and multirole combat aircraft
