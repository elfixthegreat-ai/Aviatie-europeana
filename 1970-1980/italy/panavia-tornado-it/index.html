Panavia Tornado

Manufacturer: Panavia Aircraft GmbH (Aeritalia of Italy / British Aerospace / MBB of West Germany)
Country / decade: Italy, 1970–1980
Category: Military
Role: Variable-sweep-wing strike and multirole combat aircraft
First flight: 14 August 1974
Entered service: 1981, with the Italian Air Force

Design and development

Aeritalia's wing-manufacturing role gave Italian industry responsibility for one of the Tornado's most technically demanding structural elements, since the variable-sweep pivot mechanism had to remain reliable through tens of thousands of wing-sweep cycles over an aircraft's operational life. Italy's later procurement of the specialised electronic-warfare Tornado ECR variant, developed jointly with West Germany in the 1980s, grew directly out of the design and manufacturing expertise Aeritalia built up through this original 1970s programme.

Where it was used, and by whom

Italy held a 15% workshare of the trinational Panavia programme, building the Tornado's wings at Aeritalia; Italian Air Force deliveries began in September 1981, just after the close of this site's period, with the type developed throughout the 1970s.

Notes

See the United Kingdom's 1970s Tornado entry for the shared programme background. Italy's smaller workshare, relative to the UK and West Germany, reflected its smaller initial aircraft order.
