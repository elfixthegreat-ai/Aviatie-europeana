Netherlands · 1970–1980

The F28 Fellowship entered widespread airline service through this decade, engineered to use around 85 percent of the same short or unprepared airfields as its turboprop predecessor — extending Fokker's regional-aircraft specialism into the jet era rather than replacing it. Garuda Indonesia became its largest single operator, alongside carriers across Scandinavia and South America.

Dutch industry also partnered with West Germany on the VFW 614 regional jet, a joint venture that ultimately sold poorly and was cancelled by 1977 — a reminder that not every collaborative programme of this period succeeded commercially, even when the underlying engineering was sound. By the end of the decade, the F27-and-F28 family had nonetheless made Fokker one of the most successful regional-aircraft manufacturers anywhere in the world.
