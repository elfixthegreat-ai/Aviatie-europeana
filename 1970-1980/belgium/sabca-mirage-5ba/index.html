Dassault Mirage 5BA (SABCA-built)

Manufacturer: Originally Dassault Aviation (France); built under license by SABCA, Belgium
Country / decade: Belgium, 1970–1980
Category: Military
Role: Ground-attack and reconnaissance fighter
First flight: Design first flew 1967 (France); Belgian-built aircraft from 1969–70
Entered service: around 1970, with the Belgian Air Component

Design and development

The Mirage 5 series began as a private Israeli request to Dassault for a simplified, lighter-weight Mirage III optimised for ground attack rather than interception, stripped of some of the original's radar and avionics in exchange for greater range and payload; Belgium's own Mirage 5BA adapted that simplified airframe back toward a more capable, radar-equipped configuration suited to Belgium's NATO strike-and-reconnaissance role, illustrating how the same basic airframe was reshaped differently by different air forces' requirements.

Where it was used, and by whom

Ordered by Belgium in 1968 to replace ageing F-84F fighter-bombers, 106 aircraft (63 strike Mirage 5BA, 27 reconnaissance 5BR, and 16 two-seat 5BD trainers) were built at SABCA's Haren and Gosselies plants, making Belgium the largest single operator of the Mirage 5 anywhere.

Notes

Included here as a Belgian-manufactured aircraft, though the design originated with Dassault in France. SABCA's Mirage 5 programme was one of the largest single license-production runs by a smaller European aerospace industry in this period.
