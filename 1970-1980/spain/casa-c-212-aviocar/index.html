CASA C-212 Aviocar

Manufacturer: Construcciones Aeronáuticas SA (CASA)
Country / decade: Spain, 1970–1980
Category: Civil & military
Role: STOL twin-turboprop transport
First flight: 26 March 1971
Entered service: May 1974, with the Spanish Air Force

Where it was used, and by whom

The Spanish Air Force was the launch customer, replacing older piston transports; a civil version followed in 1975. The C-212 went on to serve some 90 operators across Europe, Latin America, and Southeast Asia, and was later license-built in Indonesia.

Notes

Spain's first significant original postwar transport design, the C-212 remained in continuous production in Seville for four decades and became one of the country's best-known aerospace exports.
