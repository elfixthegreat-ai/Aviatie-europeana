United Kingdom · 1970–1980

The decade was dominated by two collaborative and technologically radical programmes: the Anglo-French Concorde and the V/STOL Harrier, both eventually exported far beyond the UK.

Aircraft types covered

  • Hawker Siddeley Harrier Military — V/STOL close-support and attack aircraft (“jump jet”)
  • Panavia Tornado Military — Variable-sweep-wing strike and multirole combat aircraft

European Skies, 1950–1980 is an independent, non-commercial reference project. Aircraft illustrations are simple schematic diagrams for orientation only, not verified technical drawings or photographs — see each aircraft page for cited sources.
