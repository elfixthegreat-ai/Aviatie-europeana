Hawker Siddeley Harrier

Manufacturer: Hawker Siddeley
Country / decade: United Kingdom, 1970–1980
Category: Military
Role: V/STOL close-support and attack aircraft (“jump jet”)
First flight: 28 December 1967
Entered service: 1 April 1969, with the Royal Air Force

Where it was used, and by whom

The RAF based much of its Harrier force in West Germany, using the aircraft's ability to disperse away from vulnerable, easily-targeted conventional air bases in the event of a Warsaw Pact invasion. It was exported to the United States Marine Corps as the AV-8A, and naval derivatives (the Sea Harrier) later served with the Royal Navy, the Spanish Navy, and the Indian Navy.

Notes

The Harrier was the only truly successful vertical/short take-off and landing (V/STOL) design among the many attempted in this period, able to operate from improvised sites such as forest clearings or car parks rather than fixed runways.
