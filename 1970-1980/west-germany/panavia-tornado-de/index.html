Panavia Tornado

Manufacturer: Panavia Aircraft GmbH (MBB of West Germany / British Aerospace / Aeritalia of Italy)
Country / decade: West Germany, 1970–1980
Category: Military
Role: Variable-sweep-wing strike and multirole combat aircraft
First flight: 14 August 1974
Entered service: 1979–80, with the Luftwaffe

Design and development

West Germany's Luftwaffe and Marineflieger needed the Tornado to replace both the F-104G Starfighter's strike role and older Lockheed F-104 and Fiat G.91 aircraft in a single new type, reflecting the aging and increasingly costly patchwork of Cold War-era aircraft German industry had accumulated across the 1950s and 1960s. Germany's equal workshare with Britain gave MBB engineers direct design authority over major structural and systems elements, a very different arrangement from the purely licensed production Germany had taken on with the F-104G a decade earlier.

Where it was used, and by whom

West Germany was one of the three founding Panavia partner nations, taking the centre-fuselage workshare through MBB and fielding the Tornado alongside the RAF and the Italian Air Force from 1979–80.
