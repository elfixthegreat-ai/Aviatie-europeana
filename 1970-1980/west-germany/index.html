West Germany · 1970–1980

German industry attempted its first indigenous postwar jet airliner in partnership with the Netherlands, even as it began the multinational collaborations that would lead toward Airbus and the Tornado.

Aircraft types covered

  • VFW-Fokker 614 Civil — Twin-engine regional jet airliner
  • MBB Bo 105 Civil & military — Light twin-engine multi-purpose helicopter
  • Panavia Tornado Military — Variable-sweep-wing strike and multirole combat aircraft

European Skies, 1950–1980 is an independent, non-commercial reference project. Aircraft illustrations are simple schematic diagrams for orientation only, not verified technical drawings or photographs — see each aircraft page for cited sources.
