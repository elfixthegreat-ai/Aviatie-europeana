Sweden · 1970–1980

The Saab 37 Viggen, entering service in 1971, was among the first production combat aircraft in the world to use a canard-delta configuration, and it was designed from the outset around Sweden's "Bas 60" doctrine of dispersing combat aircraft to short, camouflaged road strips rather than concentrating them on a small number of vulnerable conventional airbases — a defensive posture unique to Sweden's neutral, self-reliant strategic position.

Despite genuine interest from a United States Air Force fighter competition in the 1970s, Sweden never exported the Viggen, a decision that reflected the country's strict neutrality policy taking precedence over the commercial and diplomatic benefits an export sale might have brought. It remained, like the Tunnan two decades earlier, an aircraft built by Sweden for Sweden alone.
